ReplayHouse

A replay buffer that is just a database. The full ClickHouse engine runs as WebAssembly on this page — real tables, real weighted sampling, real training loops. Nothing leaves your machine.

01 — memory

Forgetting is the default.

The spotlight is the present. It sweeps across the photo once and never comes back. One student can only study what is happening right now. The other records every moment into a ClickHouse table — a replay buffer — and studies its memories. Watch what happens to the first student's past as the spotlight moves on.

Download the stream as a GIF

forgetting is not a metaphor here: both students update the same way — the no-buffer one simply cannot see data that is gone. the buffer is a real MergeTree table that grows as moments are lived, and the replay batches are weighted draws from it.

02 — priorities

Studying mistakes is faster.

Two identical students race to paint a photo, a batch of pixels at a time. One picks pixels at random. The other keeps a ledger of its mistakes — in a ClickHouse table running inside this tab — and asks it for the pixels it got most wrong. Same brain, same effort. First to get every patch sharp wins.

Download the race as a GIF

you can interfere: click or drag on the photo (or the ledger) to mark a region "study this now" — the brush is a real priority write, the next batch floods with those pixels, and you'll watch that patch snap into focus.

drop your own photo here to race on it — it never leaves this tab

nothing is simulated: each practice batch is a weighted draw from a real MergeTree table (this loop is prioritized experience replay — what RL and LLM post-training runs do with agent experiences instead of pixels), every error is written back as a priority, and the ledger panel is a live query over that table.
